Competitive Intelligence

Reading the giants: two ways to sell a wheelchair

While my mentor was at REHACARE, I mapped how the Big Four — Sunrise Medical, Permobil, WHILL, Pride/Quantum — ran their Aug–Sep campaigns, platform by platform.

Track A · CRT

Prescription & Reimbursement-Driven

  • Moat = clinical education for PTs / OTs / ATPs + DME dealer networks + reimbursement coding toolkits;
  • Discourse leans on biomechanics and evidence-based medicine (skin protection, shear-force elimination);
  • LinkedIn targets prescribers and channels; IG / YouTube carries athlete-ambassador brand spirit.

Sunrise Medical · Permobil · Quantum Rehab

VS
Track B · MaaS

De-medicalized Consumer EV + MaaS

  • WHILL reframed the “wheelchair” as a Personal EV — consumer-electronics-grade minimalism on IG;
  • Airport autonomous infrastructure pilots at LAX, MIA, Heathrow — a B2B smart-city narrative on LinkedIn;
  • Attracts non-paraplegic users with walking difficulties via lifestyle scenes, not clinical claims.

WHILL · Model C2 / F / R

Sunrise Medical

  • Clinical Hub CEU content for prescribers — continuing education as the channel;
  • “Team Sunrise” athlete network: 39 athletes, 15 countries;
  • Sterling S800 global debut with Scoozy on REHACARE day one — outdoor crossover scooter, 50 km range.

Permobil

  • Evidence-based-medicine moat — clinical studies over parameter sheets;
  • Exclusive munevo partnership: hands-free smart-glasses driving for ALS / MS users, exclusive in FR / IT / CH;
  • Cinematic “Impact Stories” as long-form brand assets.

Pride / Quantum

  • Brand split — Pride owns the silver-age community with warm Facebook content + Medicare Part B guidance;
  • Quantum owns CRT via iLevel seat-elevation tech;
  • “Life at iLevel” blog + veteran ambassador Bryan Anderson — equipment reframed as equal-eye-level empowerment.

WHILL

  • Consumer-electronics-grade minimalism on IG — café, airport, gallery scenes, zero clinical language;
  • B2B smart-city MaaS narrative on LinkedIn: autonomous wheelchair pilots with Envoy Air / American Airlines and ground-handling partners;
  • Completely de-labels the “wheelchair” category.
Insight → Action“These findings fed directly into our October content calendar — insight is only useful when it ships.”
中文要点

核心结论：康复出行的竞争本质不在硬件参数，而在「临床处方准入 × 医保报销通道 × 生活方式心智」的组合拳。三条可复用的规律：① 展会首日 = 新品/合作首发日，声量 48 小时内集中引爆；② 巨头以「临床循证 + 独家技术合作」构筑差异化，而非参数内卷；③ 大使叙事（Team Sunrise：15 国 39 名运动员）是可轻量复刻的长周期品牌资产。交付物：8,000+ 字中文竞品复盘报告（飞书）。

Case · @solangeb_

A para-surf champion — and how not to pitch her

French & European para-surf champion, 26K followers. Instead of the clichéd “regain your freedom” narrative, I positioned Robooter around travel efficiency between competitions — respecting her athletic identity. Product fit: E80 carbon (15.9 kg, airline-friendly).

Expand the full case file

Background — 26.1K followers, Brittany; turned to para-surf after a spinal-cord injury; French champion 2024/2025, European champion 2025.

Strategy — She already has strong athletic agency; the pitch deliberately avoided the “freedom regained” trope and opened with mobility efficiency in competition travel, car loading and daily supplementary movement.

Product match — E80 (carbon, two-step folding, airline-friendly), positioned as a long-term partner consistent with sport, inclusion and real mobility scenes.

French outreach excerpt:

« L’idée n’est pas de remplacer votre mobilité habituelle, mais d’explorer dans quelles situations une solution électrique peut devenir un vrai complément : déplacements, voyages, compétitions ou vie quotidienne. »

Negotiation & compliance — handled commission discussions beyond product exchange within a clear “promise on-site / escalate for approval” boundary; responded to a French agency’s corporate-compliance review with documentation per company process; coordinated with local dealer Ergo Concept on product-exchange logistics; flagged that the XZ-Droid Smart standing model requires professional fit confirmation for users with unclear injury levels — never promise compatibility.

中文要点

为什么用 AI 初筛而不是买名单？——垂直医疗出行赛道，场景契合度 > 粉丝量。合作原则沉淀：产品置换为主、多平台联发、优先 INS 与品牌号联合发帖、尊重达人内容自主权。达人建联的天花板不是法语语法，而是「你是否真的读过她的内容」。

02

High-ticket conversion is trust engineering, not traffic engineering.
